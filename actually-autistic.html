Actually Autistic

Run by and for autistic artists - The Gallery at City Library, Melbourne, 26 October to 17 November 2022. Rianna’s curatorial debut.

About the exhibition

Rianna’s curatorial debut as both an artist and curator participating in the exhibition. Funded by a grant given by the City of Melbourne and auspiced by Arts Access Victoria.

‘Actually Autistic: An Exhibition’ invited the people of Melbourne to join in on celebrating, nurturing and empowering autistic creativity. Run by and for autistic artists, displaying diverse and expressive artwork of the unique perspectives, experiences and voices of people on the spectrum.

‘Actually Autistic: An Exhibition’ was a collection of works aimed at cultivating understanding, celebration and representation in regards to autism. Encouraging members of the public to directly connect with and support autistic artists. All whilst challenging stereotypes by featuring the voices of marginalised genders, not often heard within our own community.

Thank you Melbourne for coming together to rejoice in individuality and neurodiversity as our artists expressed themselves freely in their chosen medium and art form in ‘Actually Autistic: An Exhibition’!
