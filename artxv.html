ARTXV

The first NFT collective for neurodivergent artists.

What ARTXV was

ARTXV is the first NFT collective for neurodivergent artists, showcasing the power of neurodiversity through works of art that show a unique perspective of the world. ARTXV works to accelerate the economic freedom of neurodivergent artists and diversify both the art and tech world.
